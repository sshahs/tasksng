## Day planner

The new *Plan* view shows your day as a timeline, with the events from your Baikal calendars next
to the tasks you plan. Today's tasks wait in a tray on the left. Drag one onto the timeline to
plan when you'll do it, drag the block to move it, drag its bottom edge to change how long it
takes, or drag it back to the tray to unplan it. On a phone, press and hold a task to pick it up.
The header adds up what is planned and how much time is free until 18:00, and ← and → move
between days.

You can also plan a task from its details with *Plan a time*. A planned time doesn't change the
task's due or start date, and it syncs to your other devices like everything else. Events are
read only and stay available offline.

*Plan* sits under *Today* in the sidebar, so the Ctrl+number shortcuts for the views and lists
below it moved down by one.

## Changed on two devices

Until now, when you edited a task on two devices before they synced, the server's version won and
the other edit was lost. Now TasksNG keeps both and asks. A banner above the list opens a side by
side comparison where you keep one version or pick fields from each. A task deleted on one device
and changed on the other is handled the same way. If both devices made the same change, nothing
is asked.

## Today widget on Android

Long-press your home screen and add the *Today* widget to see today's tasks at a glance. Tap a
task to open it, or + to add one. It moves on to the next day by itself after midnight.

Download `TasksNG_0.7.0_android.apk` below and install it over the version you have. Windows
installs update themselves as usual, and the Docker image and the Nix package are at 0.7.0 too.
