## A native Android app

The Android app is rebuilt with Android's own components (Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Material 3)
instead of a web page in a WebView. It opens faster, scrolls smoothly with hundreds of tasks, and
feels like the rest of your phone: swipe a task right to complete it and left to snooze it, pull
down to sync, and long-press for its menu. Light and dark follow the phone, and on Android 12 and
later it can take its colours from the wallpaper.

Everything the old app did is there: Today, Plan, Upcoming, Important and All tasks, your lists,
tags and saved searches with the same search filters, quick add that understands
"tomorrow 9am !1 #home every month", subtasks, Markdown notes with checklists, repeat rules,
reminders with *Snooze* and *Done*, the day planner with your calendar events, snooze, history,
choosing between versions changed on two devices, manual order, and the *Today* widget. Underneath
it is the same Rust sync engine as before, so your cache, pending changes and settings carry over.

New on Android:

- **Background sync**, so reminders added on another device are scheduled even when you don't open
  TasksNG (every 30 minutes by default; Settings → Sync in the background).
- **Overview**: today's progress, what is overdue and due this week, a streak, the last two weeks
  and open tasks per list.
- **Share to TasksNG** from any app turns the text (or a page's title and link) into a task.
- **App shortcuts** (long-press the icon) and a **Quick Settings tile** for a new task.
- Reminders come back after a restart, a time zone change or an update.
- Your password is encrypted with the Android Keystore.

## Pair your phone with the web app

The self-hosted web app and the Android app can now be paired. In the web app open *Settings →
Phones → Pair a phone* and scan the code with TasksNG on your phone (from its sign-in screen or
*Settings → Pair with the web app*): the phone signs in without typing anything. After that:

- a browser can be signed in from the phone: choose *Sign in with your phone* on the web app's
  sign-in page and scan its code;
- changes made on one show up on the other within seconds instead of at the next sync;
- *Settings → Phones* lists paired phones, and either side can unpair.

## A new look for the web app

The web app's sign-in page, the pairing screens and a new **Overview** use components from
[Aceternity UI](https://ui.aceternity.com): an aurora background, glowing bento cards and animated
borders. The Overview is in the desktop app too.

## Before you update

The Android app and the Windows updater are signed with new keys. Android only installs an update
signed like the installed app, so **uninstall TasksNG 0.8 from your phone first** (your tasks are
on your server; sign in again afterwards), then install `TasksNG_1.0.0_android.apk`. On Windows,
install `TasksNG_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` once by hand; later versions update themselves again.
