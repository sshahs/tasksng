## Custom reminders

The reminder menu has a new *Custom* section under the suggestions. Set a reminder any number of
minutes, hours, days or weeks before or after the due date (or the start date, if the task has
one), such as 90 minutes before the due time. For a task due on a day without a time, you also pick
when on that day, for example 2 days before at 07:30. Or choose *On a day* and pick a date and time
of your own. Before you add it, TasksNG shows when the reminder will go off.

## Fixes

- Typing a time below the calendar in the due date picker no longer resets while you type. The time
  is saved when you leave the field, press Enter or close the picker.
- In *Plan a time*, the Day and Time fields no longer overlap, and the reminder menu is no longer
  squeezed.
- In the planner, the line under *Today* (planned and free time) wraps onto a second line instead
  of being cut off, and the add-a-task box no longer spills out of its column.

Download `TasksNG_0.8.1_android.apk` below and install it over the version you have. Windows
installs update themselves as usual, and the Docker image and the Nix package are at 0.8.1 too.
