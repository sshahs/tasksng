## Snooze

Not now? Snooze a task and it gets out of the way until you need it. Pick *Later today*, *This
evening*, *Tomorrow*, *This weekend*, *Next week*, or a day and time of your own from the task's
right-click menu or the … menu in its details. Z snoozes the selected task until tomorrow at
09:00, and Shift+Z asks when.

A snoozed task leaves Today, the planner tray, the counts and the Android widget, and waits in a
*Snoozed* section at the bottom of its list. When the time comes it is back with a notification.
*Bring back now* ends a snooze early. Snoozing doesn't change the due date, and it syncs to your
other TasksNG devices.

## Task history

TasksNG now remembers the last 25 versions of each task: how it started, the changes you made
(edits within a few minutes of each other count as one) and the changes that came from your other
devices. Open *History…* from the task's menus, or press H, to see what changed and when. Pick an
older version to see what restoring it would change, then *Restore this version*. Undo is there
if you change your mind.

History is kept on each device and goes away with the task when you delete it.

Download `TasksNG_0.8.0_android.apk` below and install it over the version you have. Windows
installs update themselves as usual, and the Docker image and the Nix package are at 0.8.0 too.
